# Yasmine — Probability Wing Interview Prep

## Plain-English explanations to practice

**Expected value**: “If I repeated this spin thousands of times, the average nectar per spin would approach E[R].”

**MMSE**: “Given a noisy sensor Y, the best linear guess of hidden nectar X is X̂ = aY with a = σ²x/(σ²x+σ²n).”

**Poisson process**: “Events arrive randomly; average rate is λ; times between events look exponential.”

## Technical interview questions

1. How do you compute E[R] for three independent reels?
2. Why does running average converge to E[R]?
3. What happens to MSE when noise variance increases?
4. How do you simulate a Poisson number of events in [0,T]?
5. Difference between independent and uncorrelated?
