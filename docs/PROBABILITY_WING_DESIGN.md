# Probability Wing Design

## Why probability belongs in Scaly Wings

Scaly Wings is already about **transformation** and **curiosity**. Probability and stochastic processes describe how uncertainty transforms outcomes over time — the same story as a caterpillar becoming a butterfly, but in math.

## Why butterflies work as a metaphor

- **Randomness**: wind, bloom timing, predators — not fully predictable
- **Transformation**: distributions shift as stages change (larva → pupa → butterfly)
- **Structure in the long run**: one flight feels chaotic; many flights reveal λ, E[R], MSE

## The three games

| Game | Teaches |
|------|---------|
| **Butterfly Chance Garden** | PMF, independence, E[R], variance, LLN, Bayes intuition |
| **Noise Nectar Rescue** | RVs, noise, covariance, correlation, MMSE, MSE |
| **Poisson Pond Crossing** | Poisson counts, exponential inter-arrivals, stochastic traffic |
